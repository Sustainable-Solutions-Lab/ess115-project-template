# Phase 1: Question

**What goes here:** the research question your group settled on and why it is worth answering.

Put your deliverable in this folder as `question.md` (or a PDF). It should cover:

- The question, in one sentence.
- Why it is important, why it is feasible in a quarter, and why it is interesting (fun, easy, important).
- What a good answer would look like: a number, a map, a comparison?
- The first two or three sources that convinced you the question is open.
- The other questions you considered and why you set them aside.

The next group starts its literature review from this folder, so write for a reader who was not in the room.

---

*Example (the fire story in this repository):* Where in California does treating wildfire fuels return the most benefit per dollar, and is that where treatments have been happening?
